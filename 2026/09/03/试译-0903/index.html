title: 试译-0903

Hilario Ascasubi

Once there was an age of happiness
The men accepted all the calling of love and battle
With equal joy and passion.
The demon who desecrates emotion had not usurped the name of people.
In that dawn, today already ravaged,
Those years Ascasubi lived and fought,
Singing among the gauchos,
Praising for being willing to die for their homeland at just a single command.
He was many men. He was the singer and the chorus，
Along the river of history, he was Proteus.
He was a soldier in blue Montevideo
And in California, a gold seeker.
He was the joyful sparkle of a sword in the morning.
Today we are merely night and nothing.

附原文：中文版，上海译文社出版的《铁币》；西语原版。按照中文版大致翻译，欢迎交流。
